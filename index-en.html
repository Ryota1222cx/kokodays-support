Kokodays Support

Kokodays is an iPhone and Android app that works out which country you are in from your location and counts your days in each country. When a day limit you set is getting close, it lets you know with a notification. All data stays on your device, and no account is needed.

Kokodays counts days. It does not decide your tax residency or whether you meet visa or stay conditions. Check with the relevant authority or a professional before filing or applying.

FAQ

  • How days are counted: A day spent in two countries counts as one day in each. Each limit can instead count only the country you are in at the end of the day. Stays you enter take priority. Days without a location check assume you stayed in the last country seen, and are shown lighter.
  • Counting while the app is closed: Set Kokodays' location access to "Always" in your device settings. With "While Using", days are recorded when you open the app.
  • Fixing a wrong day: On the home screen, tap a country, tap the period to fix, and enter the correct country and dates. Entered stays take priority.
  • Deleting your data: Open Settings in the app and tap "Delete all data". Deleting the app also removes the data on your device.
  • Cancelling a subscription: On iPhone, open Settings, your Apple Account, Subscriptions, and cancel Kokodays. On Android, open the Google Play Store, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, and cancel Kokodays. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Moving to a new phone: Your data lives on your device, so move it with an iCloud or Google backup. Purchases can be brought back with "Restore purchases" in Settings.
