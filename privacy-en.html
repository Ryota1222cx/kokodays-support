Privacy Policy

Kokodays ("the App") is an app, provided by Ryota Masuda ("the Operator"), that counts your days in each country. This policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

1. Information kept only on your device

  • Location: Used to identify the country you are in. The App uses a border map built into the App and does not query any outside service. It stores only when you were in which country (a timestamp and a country code). It does not store latitude, longitude or routes. On Android, the operating system may briefly write coordinates to its own storage while delivering a location to the App; this is removed once processing ends.
  • Photo location and capture time: Only when you use "Rebuild from photos", the App reads where and when your photos were taken to suggest past stays. It does not read, store or send the images themselves.
  • What you enter: Stays, notes, day limits, source notes and settings.

This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Operator. The Operator runs no servers. If you use iCloud or Google backup, the operating system may include it in your backup.

2. Information sent outside the App

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): To manage purchases, the App sends an anonymous identifier it creates and your purchase records, such as the product and expiry. No email address or name is sent.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, United States): When the App stops unexpectedly, it sends the type of error, the screen name, the device model and the OS version so the problem can be fixed. Location, countries, dates, notes and time zone are not sent.
  • Apple and Google: Purchases go through the App Store and Google Play.

The App shows no ads and does no tracking for advertising.

3. Purposes

Counting days per country, notifying you before a limit, managing purchases and fixing bugs. Nothing else.

4. Deleting your data

"Delete all data" in the App's Settings removes all records, stays, limits and settings on your device. Deleting the App also removes them. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are linked to your Apple or Google purchases and cannot be deleted from the App; email us if you want them deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
