1. What the App does

The App counts your days in each country from location data and what you enter, and notifies you when a day limit you set is getting close. Day counts are estimates based on when location data arrived and on your entries. If location data does not arrive for a while, short stays or trips near borders may be missed.

The App does not decide your tax residency or whether you meet visa or stay conditions. You enter day limits yourself; the App does not suggest values. Check with the relevant authority or a professional before filing or crossing borders.
